## Scope and visitor mode
Homepage for VIP StudioS, a video and social-media studio. Persuade brands, businesses, and agencies to understand the connected offer, inspect concept work, and start an enquiry.

## Audience, job, and action
Prospective clients need a clear view of social strategy, planning, publishing, account management, video shoots, and professional editing. Lead them from the offer to services and illustrative work, then to contact.

## Proof and content
The hero pairs a clear social/video offer and project CTA with a selectable local concept frame for Social, Shoot, and Edit; the frame is explicitly not a showreel. Follow with three confirmed service lines, disclosed stock-image concept studies, a four-step brief-to-delivery process, a concise FAQ and project enquiry. Do not imply that stock images are commissioned work or promised deliverables. Keep service claims to social media strategy, planning, publishing, account management, video shoots and professional editing.

## Constraints
Use an editorial sequence with carbon black, porcelain white, and orange (#FFA000); Syne headlines, DM Sans body, accessible controls, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts. Preserve factual service descriptions, concept disclosures, enquiry behavior, SEO, and accessibility. Do not invent clients, testimonials, awards, metrics, or guaranteed outcomes. Email and WhatsApp come from the configured public environment variables.

## Direction and memorable moment
The scroll-reactive studio header contracts into a compact island, then expands on hover, keyboard focus or deliberate pin. The page follows the visitor's decision sequence: offer → confirmed services → clearly labeled concept work → process → enquiry. Orange marks primary actions and selection; it is not a performance indicator.

## Unresolved decisions
Approved showreel and portfolio assets, client permissions, founder/studio photography, confirmed service-specific deliverables, exact shoot logistics, revision/source-file policy, public prices, and any server-side enquiry endpoint or analytics provider.
