## Scope and visitor mode
Homepage for VIP StudioS, a video and social-media studio. Persuade brands, businesses, and agencies to understand the connected offer, inspect concept work, and start an enquiry.

## Audience, job, and action
Prospective clients need a clear view of social strategy, planning, publishing, account management, video shoots, and professional editing. Lead them from the offer to services and illustrative work, then to contact.

## Proof and content
The hero pairs a clear video/content offer and project CTA with a selectable local concept frame for Social, Shoot, and Edit; the frame is explicitly not a showreel. An illustrative aspect-format treatment shows the idea branching into possible content formats, followed by services, monthly support, disclosed stock-image concept studies, audience fit, a six-stage process, FAQ and project enquiry. Do not imply that stock images are commissioned work or promised deliverables.

## Constraints
Use a connected content canvas with carbon black, porcelain white, and restrained brushed gold; Manrope typography, modular media, accessible controls, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts. Preserve factual service descriptions, concept disclosures, enquiry behavior, SEO, and accessibility. Do not invent clients, testimonials, awards, metrics, or guaranteed outcomes. Email and WhatsApp come from the configured public environment variables.

## Direction and memorable moment
The Continuous Route: one unbroken gold line runs through the homepage chapters, joining the offer, services, illustrative formats, concept work, process and enquiry. Visitors can switch the concept frame between Social, Shoot and Edit, explore individual service pages, inspect disclosed concepts and send a project brief. Gold marks the route and action; it is not a performance indicator.

## Unresolved decisions
Approved showreel and portfolio assets, client permissions, founder/studio photography, confirmed service-specific deliverables, exact shoot logistics, revision/source-file policy, public prices, and any server-side enquiry endpoint or analytics provider.
